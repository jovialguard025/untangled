## 🤷‍♂️ The Big Problem: Why Normal AI Fails at Real Decisions

When people are stuck on a tough decision—like whether to change jobs, move cities, or make a big purchase—many now turn to AI chatbots like ChatGPT.

It seems helpful at first. But scientific research has revealed a big problem:

### 1. Chatbots are "Yes-Men" (Sycophancy)
Chatbots are trained to be polite and agreeable. If you say, *"I'm thinking of quitting my job to become a painter,"* the AI will usually say, *"That's so courageous! Follow your passion!"* It flatters you instead of helping you test whether you have enough savings to pay next month's rent.

### 2. Chatbots Rush You to Pick
A major 2026 study (*Nepal et al., auditing 17,930 conversations*) found that people who used AI chatbots to make career choices ended up **more doubtful and less confident** than people who just wrote their thoughts down in a notebook. Why? Because the chatbot kept pushing them to make a quick choice whenever they hesitated.

### 3. Chatbots Don't Suffer the Consequences
An AI doesn't have to live with the regret of a bad choice. **You do.**

### 1. You Are Always the Boss (Zero Advice)
Untangled will **never** tell you what to choose. It doesn't give advice, score verdicts, or tell you what's right. Instead, it asks the right questions so you can hear yourself think clearly.

### 2. Hard Limits vs. Wishes (The Non-Negotiables)
In real life, some things cannot bend:
* *"I cannot spend more than $5,000."*
* *"I must sleep at least 7 hours a night."*
* *"I cannot move away from my kids."*

In Untangled, if an option breaks your hard limit, **the app literally disables it.** No matter how glamorous an opportunity looks, if it breaks your non-negotiable rule, it gets crossed off the list. This stops "wishful thinking" in its tracks.

### 3. Escaping the "Either / Or" Trap
When people get stuck, they usually think they only have two choices:
* *"Should I quit, or stay?"*
* *"Should I buy it, or not?"*

Untangled pushes you to find a **Third Way**:
* Can you negotiate a smaller test?
* Can you try working 4 days a week?
* Can you delay the decision for 30 days and gather facts first?

Almost every big problem has a third option once you look for it.

### 4. Catching Your Own Blindspots
We all lie to ourselves a little bit. Untangled gently separates:
* **What you actually know for sure** (Facts: *"The job pays $20k more"*)
* **What you are just guessing** (Assumptions: *"I assume the new boss will be easy to work with"*)
* **What could prove you wrong** (Counter-evidence: *"The company has high employee turnover"*)

### 5. Remembering That Losing Hurts More Than Winning
Psychologists won the Nobel Prize for discovering that **losing $100 feels twice as painful as finding $100 feels good** (*Loss Aversion*).

When we get excited about an idea, we forget what we will have to give up (sleep, free time, peace of mind). Untangled forces you to name the exact sacrifice upfront: *"What would you give up, even if this goes well?"* That way, there are no nasty surprises later.

## 🧮 The "Behind-the-Scenes" Math (Without the Math Headaches)

Behind the calm lavender design, Untangled uses principles from decision science:

| Scientific Concept | What It Means in Plain English |
|---|---|
| **AHP Consistency Check** | **The Contradiction Catcher.** If you say *"Health matters more than Money,"* and *"Money matters more than Free Time,"* but then you pick an option because it gives you *"Free Time over Health,"* the system flags that you're contradicting your own values. |
| **KKT Hard Limits** | **The Height Requirement on a Rollercoaster.** If you aren't tall enough, you don't get on the ride. If an option violates your non-negotiable limit, it's out. |
| **Active Inference** | **The Smart Detective.** Instead of asking 50 random questions, the system figures out the *one single question* that clears up the biggest mystery in your dilemma. |
| **Prospect Theory** | **The Reality Check.** Making sure you don't sacrifice something you love for something that only looks shiny on paper. |
