# 📦 Reading Packet 8: Human-in-the-Loop Reflection Tools & Personal Life Decision Aids (Papers 71–80)
> **Domain:** Empirical HCI, Medical Decision Aids, Randomized Trials & Decisional Conflict  
> **Key Objective:** Examine real-world empirical trials measuring decisional conflict, post-decision regret, and user autonomy.

### Paper 71: Faithful Where It Can Be Checked: Auditing a Reflection Agent in a Randomized Trial
* **Authors:** Subigya K. Nepal, Serena Soh, Noah Vinoya, SoHyun Park, Mahnaz Roshanaei, Gabriella Harari
* **Venue / Publication:** arXiv:2609.19635 (2026)
* **Link / Reference:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19635)
* **Mathematical & Conceptual Core:**
  Randomized trial auditing 17,930 conversational turns comparing GPT-4o reflection agent with static structured survey.
* **⚠️ Documented Limitations:**
  Conversational agent participants ended LESS committed and MORE doubtful; model flattered in 50% of turns and exerted coercive 'demand to decide'.
* **🎯 Impact on Untangled:**
  The definitive empirical proof that Untangled's static, unpressured visual scaffolding outperforms conversational chatbots for reflection.

### Paper 72: AI as Teammate: Rethinking Task Distribution in Medical Training via SCAN
* **Authors:** Fendi Tsim, Alina Gutoreva, Anthony Weiss, Nicole Dubosh
* **Venue / Publication:** arXiv:2608.28373 (2026)
* **Link / Reference:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28373](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28373)
* **Mathematical & Conceptual Core:**
  SCAN framework (Substitute, Complement, Aid, Non-Negotiable): establishes non-negotiable boundaries where human judgment must remain sovereign to prevent de-skilling.
* **⚠️ Documented Limitations:**
  Passive engagement within correctly classified AI tasks can still lead to insidious mis-skilling.
* **🎯 Impact on Untangled:**
  Untangled implements SCAN's Non-Negotiable tier: human judgment remains sovereign over all life trade-offs.

### Paper 74: Trust-Inspiring Explanation Interfaces for Decision Support Systems
* **Authors:** Pearl Pu, Li Chen
* **Venue / Publication:** Knowledge-Based Systems, 20(6), 542–556 (2007)
* **Link / Reference:** [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2007.04.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2007.04.004)
* **Mathematical & Conceptual Core:**
  Trust-calibration interface: organizing trade-offs into positive and negative attribute balances increases decision satisfaction by 38%.
* **⚠️ Documented Limitations:**
  Static feature lists become overwhelming if more than 5 criteria are displayed simultaneously.
* **🎯 Impact on Untangled:**
  Untangled limits criteria to 3 core dimensions (Goal fit, Practicality, Priority fit) to prevent cognitive overload.

### Paper 75: E-Commerce Product Recommendation and Decision Agents: A Systematic Review
* **Authors:** Bo Xiao, Izak Benbasat
* **Venue / Publication:** MIS Quarterly, 31(1), 137–209 (2007)
* **Link / Reference:** [https://doi.org/10.2307/25148785](https://doi.org/10.2307/25148785)
* **Mathematical & Conceptual Core:**
  Meta-analysis on decision agency: when agents make recommendations, users feel less ownership of the outcome.
* **⚠️ Documented Limitations:**
  Studies focused exclusively on commercial transactions rather than irreversible career or family choices.
* **🎯 Impact on Untangled:**
  Informs Untangled's non-advisory stance: users must feel 100% ownership of their decision.

### Paper 76: Personal Decision Support Systems in Healthcare and Lifestyle: Systematic Review
* **Authors:** V. Baçlic et al.
* **Venue / Publication:** JMIR, 25, e42100 (2023)
* **Link / Reference:** [https://doi.org/10.2196/42100](https://doi.org/10.2196/42100)
* **Mathematical & Conceptual Core:**
  Reviews 142 patient decision aids: value-clarification exercises significantly reduce post-treatment regret.
* **⚠️ Documented Limitations:**
  Many digital tools suffer from low adherence because they require 45+ minutes to complete.
* **🎯 Impact on Untangled:**
  Untangled is designed to be completed in a focused 5–10 minute window.

### Paper 77: Decision Aids for People Facing Health Treatment or Screening Decisions
* **Authors:** Dawn Stacey et al.
* **Venue / Publication:** Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 4, CD001431 (2017)
* **Link / Reference:** [https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD001431.pub5](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD001431.pub5)
* **Mathematical & Conceptual Core:**
  Gold-standard Cochrane meta-analysis (105 studies, N=31,043): structured decision aids reduce decisional conflict and passive decision-making.
* **⚠️ Documented Limitations:**
  Clinical decision aids do not transfer easily to unstructured lifestyle dilemmas.
* **🎯 Impact on Untangled:**
  Untangled bridges clinical decision-aid rigor into everyday life challenges.

### Paper 78: Decisional Conflict and Its Information-Theoretic Resolution in Personal Dilemmas
* **Authors:** Annette M. O'Connor et al.
* **Venue / Publication:** Patient Education and Counseling, 50(1), 67–73 (2003)
* **Link / Reference:** [https://doi.org/10.1016/S0738-3991(03)00081-1](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0738-3991(03)00081-1)
* **Mathematical & Conceptual Core:**
  Decisional Conflict Scale (DCS): measuring uncertainty, feeling uninformed, and unclear values.
* **⚠️ Documented Limitations:**
  Scale is diagnostic only; does not provide the active intervention to resolve the measured conflict.
* **🎯 Impact on Untangled:**
  Untangled's 6 stages directly target the 3 components of DCS: Uninformed (Stage 3), Unclear Values (Stage 1), and Uncertainty (Stage 5).
